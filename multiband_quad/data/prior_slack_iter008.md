## Phase convention and resolution
For h=exp(-i Psi), -(1/2pi)d arg(h)/df=t_lm=t_c-tau_lm, not positive time-to-merger. We differentiate raw t_c=0 modes; tau_lm=+(1/2pi)d arg(h_lm)/df. Positive class lag is tau_slow-tau_fast=t_fast-t_slow, matching PairwiseHH. Raw phases are unwrapped on a grid uniform in f^(-5/3) with at least 8 samples per Newtonian cycle plus 8192 logarithmic frequencies, differentiated using nonuniform central differences and smoothed with an 11-point cubic Savitzky-Golay filter. Samples below 1e-6 of each mode's peak amplitude are excluded before unwrapping. Exactly zero modes at equal mass have undefined phase; no measured track is assigned. The full nonzero supported frequency range is audited, including merger/mixing, so tiny Newtonian denominators at high frequency can give large or negative ratios. These ratios are diagnostics, not a claim that Newtonian inspiral tracks describe ringdown. ToyModes recovery within 2% is a unit test; one midpoint per configuration is also checked at twice the phase resolution. This audit measures stationary tracks, not window tails.
